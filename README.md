# 💊 Blob — macOS Notch Utility Releases

Welcome to the official release repository for **Blob**, a premium, native macOS notch-based utility for offline file conversions, clipboard history, and productivity widgets.

---

## 🚀 How to Install

Installing **Blob** is incredibly simple and takes under 10 seconds:

1. **Download the Installer:** Click on the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/rendomnet/blob-releases/releases/latest)** in the sidebar and download **`Blob.dmg`**.
2. **Mount the Disk Image:** Double-click the downloaded `Blob.dmg` file on your Mac.
3. **Install:** Drag the **`Blob.app`** icon directly into your **`Applications`** folder.
4. **Launch:** Open your Applications folder (or use Spotlight search with `Cmd + Space`) and double-click **`Blob`**.
   * *Note: Since Blob is a notch-resident application, it will appear directly behind your physical Mac camera notch! Simply hover or click on the top-center of your screen to reveal it.*

---

## ✨ Features

* **📦 File Drop Zone:** Drag and drop files directly into your notch to instantly convert between formats (Images, PDFs, Audios, Archives) completely offline.
* **📋 Clipboard History:** Access your recently copied texts and links cleanly, with instant search.
* **📅 Calendar Widget:** View your upcoming events and schedule directly from your notch.
* **🎵 Media Controller:** Control Spotify or Apple Music playback beautifully from the top of your screen.
* **⏱️ Pomodoro Timer:** Keep track of your deep focus blocks with a sleek native visualizer.
* **😀 Emoji Picker:** Search and copy emojis instantly.

---

## 🔒 100% Offline & Private
* **Zero Telemetry:** We collect zero metrics, tracking, or personal data.
* **100% Local Processing:** All file conversions, metadata parsing, and widgets run entirely offline on Apple's native frameworks (`ImageIO`, `PDFKit`, `AVFoundation`).
